Problem Discovery

Existing UX & Problems

The existing experience presented several hurdles that prevented citizens from confidently using the platform. Four systemic issues emerged clearly.

The legacy portal — dense, inconsistent, and overwhelming for non-tech-savvy citizens
Unclear & Complex
Regulatory portals are often overwhelming. The existing user journeys were difficult for non-tech-savvy home buyers to navigate with confidence.
Noisy & Dated
The legacy system was riddled with inconsistent patterns, cluttered data presentation, and built on significant technical debt.
Cold & Bureaucratic Tone
Users from rural homebuyers to urban developers felt intimidated by the dense, impersonal bureaucratic interface that never considered their perspective.
Lack of Scalability
An incoming design system was needed to modernize the aesthetic, improve development velocity, and cater to future integrations across services.
Discovery Phase

Qual & Quant Research

A mixed-methods research approach helped surface both the emotional reality of users and the data patterns driving abandonment across key flows.

Part Two · The Solution Space
From insights to action
With the problem clearly framed through research, the team moved to hypothesis formation, principled design decisions, and rapid iterative prototyping.
Strategic Direction

Hypothesis & Goals

Through comprehensive user journeys and responsive design, develop a simplified, inclusive, and scalable experience that helps citizens navigate complex real estate data with confidence.

Design Foundation

Guiding Principles

Humanized
Shift away from a cold, bureaucratic feel using clearer messaging and readable typography.
Clear & Simple
Create a clean, intuitive interface that minimizes cognitive load and reduces steps.
Inclusive
Ensure the platform is accessible to all users regardless of digital literacy.
Scalable
Test and iterate on cross-functional components using atomic design principles.
Results & Conclusion

Outcome & Closing Thoughts

The revamped MahaRERA platform significantly enhanced the digital public infrastructure, bridging the gap between citizens and critical regulatory data.
