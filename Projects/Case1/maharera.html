Lean UX Step 01 01

Project Context & Case Study Setup

Define the product story before showing the process.

🎯 Purpose

A UX case study needs a clear frame: what problem existed, who was affected, what your role was, what constraints shaped the work, and what outcome the team pursued.

🧩 Step-by-step activities

  • Write a short product overview: product name, domain, platform, audience, and business context.
  • State the challenge in one concise paragraph without jumping to a solution.
💡 What to explain Explain why the project mattered, why the problem was worth solving, what you owned personally, and what limitations shaped the process.
Lean UX Step 02 02

Role & Responsibilities

Leading design end-to-end across a government-scale product.

🎯 Purpose

Balancing creative vision with regulatory accuracy and cross-functional partnership.

🧩 Step-by-step activities

  • Lead UX Designer: Led design sprints, collaborated with government stakeholders, facilitated user workshops.
  • End-to-End Design: Crafted the information architecture, user experiences, wireframes, prototypes, and visual design components.
  • Collaboration: Partnered with engineering, data teams, and policy makers to simplify complexities without losing legal accuracy.
💡 What to explain Explain what you owned personally, and how you interacted with constraints like timeline, accessibility, and legal boundaries.

Lean UX Step 04 04

Existing UX & Problems

The hurdles preventing citizens from confidently using the platform.

🎯 Purpose

Four systemic issues emerged clearly that made the existing user journeys difficult for non-tech-savvy home buyers to navigate with confidence.

🧩 Identified Issues

  • Unclear & Complex: Regulatory portals are often overwhelming. Journeys were difficult for non-tech-savvy users.
  • Noisy & Dated: The legacy system was riddled with inconsistent patterns and cluttered data presentation.
  • Bureaucratic Tone: Users felt intimidated by the dense, impersonal bureaucratic interface.
💡 What to explain Explain why this was the right problem to solve now and what you intentionally chose not to solve.
Lean UX Step 05 05

Qual & Quant Research

Learn enough to reduce uncertainty, not everything possible.

🎯 Purpose

A mixed-methods research approach helped surface both the emotional reality of users and the data patterns driving abandonment across key flows.

🧩 Step-by-step activities

  • User Interviews: Generative sessions with homebuyers & developers to understand behaviors.
  • Systems Audit: UI standards review against WCAG guidelines.
  • Data Analysis: Reviewing drop-off rates on registration flows.
💡 What to explain Do not only say “we interviewed users.” Explain who you spoke to, why, what you learned, and how it changed the direction.
Lean UX Step 06 06

Hypothesis & Goals

Convert ideas into testable learning loops.

🎯 Purpose

Through comprehensive user journeys and responsive design, develop a simplified, inclusive, and scalable experience that helps citizens navigate complex real estate data with confidence.

🧩 Strategic Direction

💡 What to explain Describe what result would change your mind. This shows maturity and avoids confirmation bias.
Lean UX Step 07 07

Guiding Principles

Design foundation that builds trust and inclusivity.

🎯 Purpose

To define a consistent, human-centered approach across all new UI components.

🧩 Core Principles

  • Humanized: Shift away from a cold, bureaucratic feel using clearer messaging and readable typography.
  • Clear & Simple: Create a clean, intuitive interface that minimizes cognitive load and reduces steps.
  • Inclusive: Ensure the platform is accessible to all users regardless of digital literacy.
  • Scalable: Test and iterate on cross-functional components using atomic design principles.
💡 What to explain Show why these principles were selected based on evidence, and how they directly map to fixing user pains.
Lean UX Step 08 08

Outcome & Closing Thoughts

End with impact, learning, and maturity.

🎯 Purpose

The revamped MahaRERA platform significantly enhanced the digital public infrastructure, bridging the gap between citizens and critical regulatory data.

💡 Closing Reflection "Designing for the public sector taught me that true impact lies in making the complex accessible and empowering to everyone — not just those comfortable with technology."
