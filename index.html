About Me

Designing with empathy, building with precision.

User Research Wireframing Prototyping Design Systems Interaction Design Usability Testing

I'm Ganesh Javhare, a UX Designer with 6+ years of experience turning complex problems into elegant, intuitive experiences. I believe great design lives at the intersection of deep user empathy and technical craft.

Previously at government authorities and a handful of early-stage startups, I've designed products used by over 6M+ users. I bring a research-led, systems-thinking approach to every project.

How I Work

My Design Process

A structured approach that keeps users at the centre of every decision.

1
Discover

Deep-dive research: user interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder alignment to understand the real problem.

2
Define

Synthesising insights into clear problem statements, personas, and design principles to guide decisions.

3
Design

Rapid ideation, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping — iterating quickly based on feedback.

4
Deliver

Usability testing, design handoff, and ongoing collaboration with engineering for quality implementation.
