How I Work

My Design Process

A structured approach that keeps users at the centre of every decision.

1.
Discover

Deep-dive research: user interviews, competitive analysis, and stakeholder alignment to understand the real problem.

2
Define

Synthesising insights into clear problem statements, personas, and design principles to guide decisions.

3
Design

Rapid ideation, wireframing, and high-fidelity prototyping — Usability Testing and iterating quickly based on feedback.

4
Deliver

Design handoff, and ongoing collaboration with engineering for quality implementation.

AI-Augmented Workflow

Designing with AI at the speed of thought.

I leverage advanced AI models and "vibe coding" methodologies to rapidly prototype ideas, iterate on logic, and bridge the gap between static design and functional reality.

🧠

Collaborative Ideation

Using LLMs as sparring partners to explore edge cases, generate copy, and structure complex information architectures.

⚡

Vibe Coding Prototypes

Translating UX concepts into interactive, code-based prototypes using AI assistants to test real interactions instantly.

🔍

Data-Driven Empathy

Synthesizing massive amounts of user research and qualitative feedback rapidly to extract actionable UX insights.
